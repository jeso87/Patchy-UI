### A tidier site page

- **One row of actions** beside the site's name: **Copy Address**, **Open in Browser**, **Share**, and a **…** menu with Reveal in Finder and Remove Site.
- **Today at a glance.** Each site's page now counts today's errors and visits. Click either one to jump straight to that log.
- **A more compact sidebar.** Each site takes one line, and a mark appears only beside sites that need attention. Certificate details live on the site's page.

### Server controls in one menu

Click **Running · PHP 8.5** in the toolbar to restart or stop the server. When more than one version of PHP is installed, you can also switch between them there.

### Every way to share

Patchy 1.4.0 adds new ways to share a site, and Share now offers all of them once you've connected your own domain:

- **Your site's own address** on your domain, open to anyone or **with a password**.
- **Change Address…** picks the name before your domain, such as `preview.example.com`. Patchy remembers it for next time.
- **A random address** on your domain that is new each time, open or with a password.
- **A public link** on trycloudflare.com, even when a domain is connected.

To stop using your domain, go to **Settings › General** and choose **Disconnect**.

Made for **Patchy 1.4.0**. Update Patchy first, in Terminal: `brew upgrade joeworkman/patchy/patchy`
