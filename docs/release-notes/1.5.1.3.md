### Fixes

- **Try Again shares the way you chose.** If a share failed, Try Again could make a different kind of link: with your own domain connected, a failed public trycloudflare.com link came back as the site's address on your domain. It now retries with the same choice.
- **Firewall apps.** When a share can't get a link, the message now says that a firewall app such as Little Snitch may need to allow cloudflared to connect.

Still made for **Patchy 1.5.1**.
