### New

- Patchy UI now speaks 36 languages. It follows the language set for it in System Settings › General › Language & Region. Patchy's own messages, under Show Details, stay in English.
- **About Patchy UI** in the Patchy UI menu now shows the Patchy, Apache and PHP versions on your Mac. **Check for Updates…** is also in Settings › General.

### Fixes

- Choosing **Not Now** when offered password-free mode no longer cancels what you were doing. The change goes ahead and asks for your Mac password.
- Dragging a site onto a group in the sidebar now works anywhere on the group, not only on its header.
- In a narrow window, the buttons above a site no longer cut off their labels.

Still made for **Patchy 1.2.1**.
