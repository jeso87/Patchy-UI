### A new menu bar panel

The menu bar menu is now a panel built for getting to your sites fast.

- **Search as you type.** Type part of a site's name and press Return to open it in your browser. The arrow keys move through the list.
- **Open it from anywhere with a keyboard shortcut.** Set one in Settings › General, and you can find and open any site without touching the mouse.
- **Sites that need attention come first,** then your groups, then everything else.
- **Server controls at the top** (Start, Stop, Restart) and **quick buttons at the bottom** for adding a site, Settings and opening Patchy UI.
- **Shared sites show in the panel too,** with Copy Link and Stop Sharing.

### Share a site at a public link

Show a client a preview without uploading anything. Click **Share** on any site and you get a link that works until you stop sharing.

- **Use your own domain.** Once you've connected one with `patchy share setup`, Share asks each time: your site's own address on that domain, **with a password** or **without one**, or a **temporary address** that is new each time.
- **Passwords are made for you,** new each time you share. **Copy Address and Password** puts everything your client needs on the clipboard, ready to paste into a message.
- **Links stop when you stop.** Sharing ends when you click Stop Sharing, stop the server or quit the app, and Patchy removes the address from your domain.

### Under the hood

- Patchy UI now asks Patchy for everything through its new machine-readable output instead of reading Patchy's files, so future Patchy releases are much less likely to break it.
- Changing a site's folder now uses Patchy's own `patchy folder` command.
- While a change runs, the app shows one step instead of a checklist, because Patchy now reports only the result.

Made for **Patchy 1.3.0**. Update Patchy first, in Terminal: `brew upgrade joeworkman/patchy/patchy`
